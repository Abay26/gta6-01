# Solmar Bay — an open-world crime story

A browser open-world crime game set in the coastal city of Solmar Bay. You play Rico Vega, a getaway driver pulled back into the life by fixer Mara Solis. Take jobs, outrun the law, and carve out a place of your own in the city.

## The story

Ten story jobs for Mara Solis take Rico from small-time pickups to high-stakes heists across all four districts. Each job pays out and pushes the story forward — new contacts, new heat, new ways to spend the money.

## The city

Four districts to explore, each with its own look and feel:

- **Old Town** — narrow streets, old brick, tight corners.
- **Downtown** — towers, traffic, and the busiest streets in Solmar Bay.
- **Northgate Hills** — winding roads with long sightlines and fast straights.
- **Marina** — the waterfront, docks, and the boats.

A full day/night cycle and changing weather roll across the city, with traffic and pedestrians moving through the streets around you.

## Driving and getting around

Steal and drive cars, hop on bikes, or take a speedboat out from the Marina. Handling, collisions, and the city layout make every escape route a choice — a shortcut through Old Town can save you, or trap you.

## Wanted level

Heat runs from one to five stars. The higher the heat, the harder the police come after you — and at four and five stars a police helicopter joins the chase, tracking you from above until you break line of sight and cool off.

## Combat

Fights break out on foot and from behind the wheel. Take cover, pick your moments, and don't start something you can't finish — every shot draws attention.

## Phone, money, and gear

Press **Q** to open the in-game phone for jobs, contacts, and messages. Money earned from jobs can be spent on a pistol, new jackets, fresh repaints for your rides, and the Marina Loft safehouse — your own place to lay low and stash what matters.

## Radio

Three synthesized radio stations play as you drive, each with its own mood, so the city never sounds the same twice.

## Interface and saves

The HUD keeps your health, cash, wanted level, and objectives in view, with a minimap tracking the streets, markers, and pursuit around you. Progress saves automatically, so you can pick up right where Rico left off.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser — no install needed. Drive with the keyboard, use the phone with **Q**, and follow the job markers to start each story mission.
